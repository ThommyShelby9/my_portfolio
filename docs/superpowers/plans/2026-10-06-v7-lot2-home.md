# Portfolio v7 « Digital DNA », Lot 2 : accueil en six scènes : Implementation Plan

> Executed inline by the controller (owner instruction 2026-10-06: no subagents). Steps use checkbox syntax.

**Goal:** Turn the home into the six-scene DNA journey of spec §4.1: the helix morphs with the scroll from formation to sequencing, construction, expression, lab and stabilisation, over real HTML content.

**Architecture:** `buildLayout` gains four more states (layers, signatures, clusters, calm) and a group index; the shader blends any two consecutive states from one `uState` uniform and highlights the focused group. `DnaTrajectory` (GSAP ScrollTrigger, lazy 3D chunk only) maps `data-dna-scene` sections and `data-dna-focus` blocks to `uState` / `uFocus`. On the home, the live stage is fixed behind the content (text left, helix right on desktop; faded background on phones). Gene data and project genes come from typed, verified sources.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-06-portfolio-v7-digital-dna-design.md` §3.4, §3.5, §3.7, §4.1, §5.3, §5.4, §6.

## Global Constraints

Same as Lot 1 (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-06-v7-lot1-foundations-dna.md`, Global Constraints), plus:
- Gene ids: `engineering, product, architecture, innovation, devops, leadership`; pairs in order Engineering↔Product, Architecture↔Innovation, DevOps↔Leadership.
- Every project has 2 to 4 genes, no duplicates, identical in FR and EN files.
- Pair proofs and construction steps use only verified facts (content files, cv-data, owner facts); every new sentence goes on the owner validation list.
- The helix state is decorative: every scene is complete and readable without JS, without WebGL and under reduced motion. « Séquencer » is a native `<details>`/`<summary>` (works without JS).

## Decisions

- State indices: 0 cloud, 1 helix, 2 layers, 3 signatures, 4 clusters, 5 calm. Final position = `mix(cloud, blend(floor(uState), ceil(uState)), intro)`; the intro runs once on load (time), the scroll moves `uState` between 1 and 5 with a 1.1 s GSAP tween per scene change (continuous, never hijacks the native scroll).
- Group index `aGroup` (per particle, integer 0..59): layers use `aGroup % 5`, signatures `% 3`, clusters `% 4`; the helix uses `aPair`. `uFocus` highlights the group of the current state in signal orange.
- Layers: 5 horizontal discs, top = Idée. Signatures: 3 horizontal rose rings at y = 2.6, 0, −2.6 (featured projects in order). Clusters: 4 small spheres. Calm: helix radius × 0.8.
- In states ≥ 2 the group leans 0.35 rad towards the camera so horizontal shapes read as ellipses.
- Signature shape: `r(θ) = R · (1 + 0.16 · Σ_g cos(k_g θ + φ_g))`, `k_g = index(g) + 2`, `φ_g = index(g) · 0.7`, normalised; the same pure function gives the 3D points and the SVG path (`ProjectSignature`).
- Header nav « Expertise » becomes « ADN » / « DNA » → `/#adn` (scene 01).
- Conversion title becomes « Prêt à construire. » / « Ready to build. »
