HR software is demanding: a wrong payroll calculation means an underpaid employee and a social security return to correct. I build HR tools that are reliable, tested, and able to follow regulatory changes without a rewrite.

## What I deliver

- **Payroll**: salaries, contributions and deductions, payslips generated as PDF, full history.
- **Social security returns**: CNSS returns prepared with configurable calculation rules.
- **Personnel management**: employee records, contracts, leave and absences, documents.
- **Multi-company** for HR firms and groups: separated data, per-company roles, consolidated view.
- **Time tracking**: a mobile app for geolocated clock-ins that works offline.
- **Simulators**: on EasyToWork, a salary simulator produces its PDF right in the browser.

## HR tools already shipped

**TadagbeRhPlus** is the multi-company HR system of an HR consultancy: payroll, leave, contracts and CNSS returns in one tool. I modernised it without downtime, with 85% less manual data entry and three CNSS regulatory updates without a single regression.

**Ubbfy** is an ERP whose rebuild I led around a single Django API: employees, payroll, recruitment, invoicing, and a Flutter app for geolocated clock-ins.

**Freelance Club** runs umbrella employment, from the assignment to the payslip and the payment.

## Technologies

Django and Python, Laravel, Vue 3, PostgreSQL and MySQL, Celery and RabbitMQ for bulk calculations, PDF generation, Flutter for mobile.
