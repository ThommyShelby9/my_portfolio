I have been designing and shipping SaaS platforms for six years, three of them as tech lead. Currently Head of Engineering & Innovation at KPS Groupe, I also take freelance work on B2B products: operations, human resources, payments, contracts.

## What I deliver

- **The product architecture**: modules, a multi-tenant data model, roles and permissions, and a stack chosen for your team and budget.
- **The back end and API**: Django and Django REST Framework, Laravel, Node.js and Express, Spring Boot. Background jobs with Celery, RabbitMQ or Bull, Redis caching.
- **The front end**: Vue 3, Nuxt, React and Next.js, in TypeScript.
- **Billing and payments**: subscriptions, Mobile Money (FedaPay, Kkiapay, PayPlus), webhooks verified on the server.
- **Production**: Docker, continuous integration, monitoring, backups, and automated tests so that every release stays safe.

## SaaS products already shipped

**ContractIQ** is a B2B SaaS that reads contracts with AI, extracts their risks and deadlines, and warns before every renewal: 52 data models, about 213 API routes and 44 unit test files.

**TadagbeRhPlus** is the multi-company HR system of an HR consultancy. I modernised it without downtime: manual data entry dropped by 85%, and three CNSS regulatory updates shipped without a single regression.

**Freelance Club**, an umbrella-company platform, follows a freelancer from the assignment to the payslip. **Upgrade Afrique** went to production in one month, from architecture to deployment.

## How I work

1. **Scoping**: a call to understand the business, the users and the constraints, then a quote split into phases.
2. **Short iterations**: a usable version every one or two weeks, with a demo.
3. **Quality from day one**: automated tests, code review, a staging environment.
4. **Handover**: documentation and full access to the code and infrastructure. The product is yours.

## Who it is for

Startups launching a first product, small and mid-sized companies replacing spreadsheets with a tailored tool, and software vendors who need a tech lead to take over an existing platform.
