This site presents the work of Rostel Panoumassi, a product engineer based in Cotonou. It collects very little data, and only what you choose to send. This page explains what, why, where it is kept and how to exercise your rights.

## Data controller

Rostel Panoumassi, Cotonou, Benin. For any question about your data: [rmissimawu@gmail.com](mailto:rmissimawu@gmail.com).

## Data collected

The site only collects personal data when you fill in one of its two forms.

**The project brief** (Brief page):

- the type of need, the project description, its current stage and the size of your team;
- the skills already in the team (developers, designer, product owner);
- free-form details, the preferred deadline and, if you wish, a budget range;
- your first name, last name and email address;
- if you provide them: your company, your website and how you found the site;
- whether you would rather start with a call.

**The contact form** (Contact page): your name, your email address and your message.

In both cases, the language of the page is saved with your submission so the reply comes in the same language. Neither your IP address nor your browser details are saved with your answers.

## Purpose and legal basis

Your answers are used only to study your request, reply to you and follow up on our exchanges. They are never sold, rented or used for marketing, and no automated decision is made from them.

The legal basis is taking pre-contractual steps at your request (a brief or a quote request) and, for a simple message, the legitimate interest in replying to people who write.

## Protection against abuse

To limit automated submissions, the server accepts at most 5 submissions per hour from the same connection. To do so it uses your IP address, in memory only and only for that check: it is never saved or shared. A hidden field also traps bots that fill in every field.

## Recipients and hosting

Only Rostel Panoumassi has access to your answers. They are kept in two places:

- in a Google Cloud Firestore database (Firebase project), hosted by Google in the United States (nam5 multi-region location). Google covers this transfer outside Benin and the European Union with the standard contractual clauses included in its data processing terms;
- in Rostel Panoumassi’s mailbox, which receives a copy of each submission through an email delivery provider (SMTP).

The site itself is hosted on a private server administered by Rostel Panoumassi.

## Retention

Your answers are kept for 24 months after our last exchange, then deleted. You can ask for them to be deleted sooner at any time.

## Cookies and audience measurement

The site sets no cookie and uses no advertising tracker or third-party analytics tool. It loads no external script.

To know which pages are read, the site keeps a simple, anonymous daily count. For each page view it saves only:

- the page address (for example /en/work);
- the name of the site you came from, when it is another site (for example google.com);
- the country, only when the network provides it;
- the day’s total of page views.

No IP address, no identifier and no information that could recognise you from one visit to the next is saved. These figures are stored in the same Firestore database.

## Security

The database is closed to any public access: only the site’s server can write to it, with a private access key kept on that server. Traffic to the site is encrypted (HTTPS).

## Your rights

You can ask to access your data, correct it, delete it, receive a copy in a common format, or object to its processing. Simply write to [rmissimawu@gmail.com](mailto:rmissimawu@gmail.com) from the email address used in the form. You will receive an answer within one month at the latest.

This processing falls under the Digital Code of the Republic of Benin and, for visitors in the European Union, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). If you believe your rights are not respected, you can lodge a complaint with Benin’s personal data protection authority, the Autorité de Protection des Données à caractère Personnel ([APDP](https://apdp.bj)), or, if you live in the European Union, with the data protection authority of your country.

## Changes

This policy may change with the site. The date of the last update is shown at the top of this page.
