Unsolicited redesign proposal, made to show what Procom could become.

## The challenge

Procom is a full-service communication agency: strategy, branding, influence, video production, consulting, reputation management, crisis communication. A typical agency site lines these up in a grid of cards and ends up looking like every other agency.

The idea behind this proposal fits in one sentence: Procom does not just do communication, it shapes influence. The site had to make people feel that before saying it, in an editorial, controlled and lively tone.

## Key features

- **An opening on the influence flow**: an animated orbital mark that sets out the central idea on arrival.
- **A manifesto** stating the agency’s position.
- **“Seven forces, one flow”**: the seven trades presented as parts of a single movement, not as a catalogue.
- **A four-step method**: strategy, narrative, propagation, measurement.
- **Client cases, expertise and contact**, carrying the story through to getting in touch.

## Constraints and decisions

- **A story in acts, not a grid.** The page reads top to bottom like a narrative, each section setting up the next. Scrolling becomes the storytelling.
- **Motion in service of reading.** GSAP drives the reveals and Lenis smooths the scroll. Scrolling is never hijacked: visitors move at their own pace.
- **Motion preferences respected.** When the system asks for reduced motion, scroll smoothing is turned off, the orbital mark stays still, and the stylesheets neutralise transitions.

## Outcome

A working Next.js proposal that shows the agency a site that lives up to its own message: a story rather than a brochure.
